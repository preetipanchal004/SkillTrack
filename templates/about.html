ABOUT SKILLTRACK

Build Skills. Track Progress. Get Placement Ready.

SkillTrack is a student skill development and placement analytics website designed to help students understand their current preparation level and improve their career readiness.

What is SkillTrack?

SkillTrack provides a centralized platform where students can manage their skills, courses, certifications, projects, aptitude performance and interview preparation.

The system analyzes student progress and provides placement readiness, skill gap analysis and personalized recommendations.

Our Objective

The main objective of SkillTrack is to help students identify their strengths and weaknesses before entering the placement process.

By combining student data with analytics, the system helps students make better decisions about the skills they need to improve.
